### Buying Guide

Q: What are the most important rules when buying a new phone?
A: Set a budget; decide whether camera, battery or gaming matters most; check software-update support; buy with a GST bill from a trusted seller; and inspect the phone in person before deciding.

Q: Should I buy the latest model or last year's?
A: Buy the latest model if its new features matter to you. A previous-generation model may offer similar everyday performance at a lower price. Compare remaining software-support years, specifications and current prices.

Q: What accessories should I buy with a new phone?
A: A screen guard and back cover are useful basics. Choose a good-quality charger and cable compatible with the phone. A power bank may help heavy users. Avoid poor-quality chargers.

Q: What should I check on a used phone before buying?
A: Check the IMEI, display, touch, cameras, speakers, charging port, battery condition, Wi-Fi, SIM slots and buttons. Ask for the original bill and box if available. Ensure the phone is not locked to another person's Google or Apple account, and get a receipt with the seller's details.

Q: Which mobile brand is best?
A: The best brand depends on budget, intended use and service availability. Samsung and Apple are known for software support; Redmi and Realme offer budget-focused options; Vivo and OPPO are popular for camera features; OnePlus and iQOO are often considered for performance and gaming. Compare specific models rather than choosing by brand alone.

### Phone Specs

Q: How much RAM do I actually need?
A: 4 GB may suit basic use such as calls, WhatsApp and YouTube. 6–8 GB suits many everyday users and moderate gaming. 12 GB or more may help heavy gaming and multitasking. Actual performance also depends on the processor and software optimisation.

Q: Which phone has the best camera?
A: Camera performance depends on the exact model and the type of photography or video. iPhone and Samsung Galaxy models are strong all-round options; Vivo and OPPO often focus on portraits and selfies; Redmi and Realme have options at lower budgets. Compare sample photos and video from the models you are considering.

Q: Which brand is best for gaming?
A: Gaming performance depends on the model, processor, cooling, display and battery—not brand alone. Look for a capable Snapdragon 7/8-series or comparable Dimensity processor, sufficient RAM, a high-refresh-rate display and good thermal management.

Q: Fast charging or big battery — which matters more?
A: Battery capacity affects how long a phone lasts, while charging speed affects how quickly it recharges. Choose based on your daily usage and check the actual battery performance and supported charging speed of the model.

Q: Is AMOLED better than LCD?
A: AMOLED/OLED generally offers deeper blacks and higher contrast, which can be good for video and gaming. LCD may be suitable for budget devices and can have its own visibility advantages. The better choice depends on budget and use.

Q: How important is the processor?
A: The processor affects speed, gaming, multitasking and how well a phone handles demanding apps and updates. For calls, social media and video, a mid-range processor may be enough. Match the processor to your actual needs rather than overspending.

### Warranty & Exchange

Q: Is warranty valid without a GST bill?
A: Warranty requirements vary by brand and service policy, but proof of purchase is commonly required. Keep the GST bill and the box showing the IMEI number where possible.

Q: What does the brand warranty cover?
A: Brand warranties commonly cover manufacturing defects for a defined period, often one year for handsets. Water damage, physical damage, dropped screens and accessories may be excluded. Check the warranty terms for the specific device. Tech Universe can help coordinate warranty claims.

Q: Can I exchange my old phone at Tech Universe?
A: Yes. Bring the old phone for an in-store evaluation; exchange value depends on its model and condition. The original box, bill and charger may help with evaluation. Screen damage can reduce value. Back up your data, factory-reset the phone and sign out of your accounts before handing it over.

Q: How do I back up data before switching phones?
A:
1. Turn on backup in your Google Account or iCloud settings.
2. In WhatsApp, open Settings → Chats → Chat Backup and select Back Up Now.
3. Make sure you can access important app accounts.
4. Use the new phone's setup wizard to restore the backup.
5. Check that your data has transferred before wiping the old phone.

Q: How do I get the highest price for my used phone?
A: A clean, working phone with its original box, bill and charger may receive a better offer. Compare more than one offer, remove your accounts, factory-reset the phone and obtain a written receipt. Contact Tech Universe with your model for an estimate; final value depends on inspection.
