This page explains what information Tech Universe collects when you visit techuniversehdb.in or interact with us in-store, how we use it, and who we share it with. We're a local mobile phone and accessories retailer in Haldibari — this policy is written in plain language on purpose, not legal boilerplate.

1. Who We Are

Tech Universe is a mobile phone and accessories retail store located at Gosala Road, Haldibari, Cooch Behar, West Bengal — 735122, India. This policy covers our website (techuniversehdb.in) and how we handle information from visitors, customers, and anyone who contacts us through it.

2. Information We Collect

Contact form. When you use the contact form on this site, we collect your name, phone number, subject, and message. This information is sent directly to us via WhatsApp when you click "Send Message" — it is not stored on our servers or any database.

WhatsApp messages. If you message us on WhatsApp (through our floating button, contact page, or any link on this site), that conversation is handled by WhatsApp/Meta under their own privacy policy. We use it only to respond to your query.

Automatically collected data. Like most websites, our hosting and content-delivery providers (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare) automatically log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and performance purposes.

In-store visits. If you visit our physical store, we do not collect any personal data beyond what's needed to process a purchase or EMI application, as described below.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to questions sent through our contact form or WhatsApp
  • To help you check stock, pricing, or EMI eligibility for a phone you're interested in
  • To improve this website and understand which pages and phones visitors are interested in
  • To meet legal or regulatory requirements applicable to our business

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone for marketing purposes.

4. EMI & Financing Partners

The EMI calculator on this site is only an estimate — it does not submit any application or personal data anywhere. If you choose to apply for EMI financing in-store, your documents (ID proof, income details, etc.) are collected on paper or through the finance partner's own app/portal, and shared directly with the finance partner you choose:

Bajaj Finserv HDB Financial Services Cholamandalam Finance TVS Credit Axio Samsung Finance+ IDFC FIRST Bank

Each of these partners is a separate financial institution with its own privacy policy and data handling practices, which apply to the information you give them directly. We recommend reviewing the relevant partner's policy before submitting an EMI application with them.

5. Third-Party Services on This Site

This site loads a small number of third-party services to function properly:

  • Google Fonts & cdnjs (Bootstrap/Bootstrap Icons) — deliver fonts and styling; may log basic request data (like IP address) per their own policies.
  • Google Maps — embedded on our Contact page to show our store location; governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Sheets / Apps Script — powers our live phone price list, FAQs, and reviews sections. These only read publicly-listed catalog/review data — no visitor data is sent to this service.
  • X (Twitter) and Instagram embeds — used to show our posts on this site. These are loaded directly from X/Meta and are subject to their respective privacy and cookie policies.
  • WhatsApp — used for our contact button and contact form; governed by WhatsApp/Meta's privacy policy.
We don't control how these third-party services handle data on their end — we recommend checking their individual privacy policies if you have concerns.

6. Cookies

This site itself does not set tracking or advertising cookies. However, embedded third-party content (Google Maps, X/Twitter, Instagram) may set their own cookies when displayed, as described above. If we add analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) in the future, this section will be updated to reflect that.

7. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We only share your information in these situations:

  • With the specific EMI finance partner you choose to apply through (see Section 4)
  • With service providers who help us operate this website (e.g. hosting), solely to provide that service
  • If required by law, court order, or government request

We do not share your contact form details, phone number, or messages with any third party for marketing.

8. Data Security

Since our contact form sends data directly through WhatsApp rather than storing it on a server, there is no central database of customer messages for us to secure or that could be breached. For in-store financing, document handling security is managed by the respective finance partner.

9. Children's Privacy

This site and our services are intended for general audiences and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your Rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you (typically limited to messages you've sent us), request that we delete it, or ask us to correct it. Since we don't maintain a customer database from this website, most requests can be resolved simply by asking us directly using the contact details below.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if we add new features like analytics or online payments. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest version.
